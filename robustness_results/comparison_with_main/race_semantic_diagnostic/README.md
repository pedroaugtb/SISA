# Race semantic-displacement diagnostic

The estimand is the subject-normalized semantic distance from the shared `People` response for
Black people minus the corresponding distance for White people. Positive values indicate that
the Black-condition response is farther from `People`; negative values indicate that the White-
condition response is farther from `People`.

## Main finding

The Dallas reversal is distributed across outcomes rather than being caused by a single item:
6/7 Dallas contrasts are negative. `Neighborhood quality` is the largest negative
contributor (-0.152), but removing it leaves the Dallas
mean negative (-0.032). None of the seven leave-one-out
analyses changes the sign of the Dallas mean.

The location means are Dallas -0.050, New York City
+0.029, and Los Angeles +0.034.
Their pooled mean is +0.004. Thus the small positive pooled estimate is produced by the
positive New York and Los Angeles means compensating for the negative Dallas mean.

This is a descriptive diagnostic, not a basis for excluding outcomes. With seven outcomes,
individual items have substantial influence, and the pooled Race effect remains close to zero.
