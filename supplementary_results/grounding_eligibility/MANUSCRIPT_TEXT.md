# Suggested manuscript text

Of the 819 primary AIO responses, 476 (58.1%)
met the reference evidence-recovery criterion of at least 50% of cited sources recovered, at least
three usable sources, and a nonmissing source-balanced grounding score. Among the 756
explicit-group responses used to construct minority--majority comparisons, 441
(58.3%) were eligible. This yielded 122 of
378 (32.3%) complete location-specific
minority--majority outcome pairs and 76 of 126 pooled
dimension--outcome pairs with at least one complete location.

The continuous blocked association shown in Figure 4.5 used 212 pooled explicit
condition--outcome means across all 21 outcomes (115
minority and 97 majority). Of these,
67 were informed by one eligible location,
61 by two locations, and
84 by all three locations.

Eligibility was 69.8% for minority-marked responses and
46.8% for majority-marked responses, a difference of
23.0 percentage points (95% outcome-bootstrap CI
[15.9, 29.9];
paired outcome-level sign-flip $p<.001$).

The eligibility difference reflects multiple components of the reference rule. The 50% recovery
threshold was met by 70.4% of
minority-marked and 56.1% of
majority-marked responses; the minimum of three recovered sources was met by
89.9% and
61.9%, respectively. Grounding
results should therefore be interpreted as conditional on evidence-recovery eligibility rather
than representative of every collected response.
